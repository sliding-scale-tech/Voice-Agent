Why not just use a spreadsheet or a virtual assistant?

A spreadsheet doesn't answer the phone at 2am, and a VA either isn't awake or costs significantly more once you actually staff for 24/7 coverage. This handles every call the moment it comes in, applies the same 5 qualifying questions every time, and logs the result automatically. No shift schedule, no coverage gaps.

What if it can't understand a caller?

After two failed attempts to clarify, it hands off to your team instead of guessing or looping forever.

What if someone asks something it doesn't know, like unit-specific details?

It tells them someone will follow up, logs the question, and doesn't make anything up.

What if there's no unit that matches what they're looking for?

It offers a waitlist or the closest available alternative. It doesn't just say no and hang up.

What about existing tenants calling about maintenance or an emergency?

Routed straight to a human, every time. This isn't a maintenance line, and it doesn't pretend to be one.

What if your system or our property management software goes down mid-call?

It still takes the caller's info manually and lets them know someone will follow up. That counts as a handled call in your dashboard, not a silent drop.

Will this replace my leasing team?

No. It handles the repetitive first conversation, the questions every caller asks, so your team spends their time touring qualified prospects and closing leases, not fielding calls at midnight or repeating the same qualifying questions forty times a week.
