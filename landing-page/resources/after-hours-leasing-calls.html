After-hours coverage

How AI Voice Receptionists Handle After-Hours Leasing Calls

Most leasing offices are staffed for about 40 hours a week. Most leasing inquiries don't arrive during those hours. That gap is where a large share of prospective renters get lost, not because a property isn't a good fit, but because nobody answered the phone when they called.

The scale of the problem

76% of leasing inquiries come in after standard business hours. When someone finally does get a response, speed matters more than most property managers expect: a caller contacted within 5 minutes is 21 times more likely to convert than one contacted after 30 minutes. Industry estimates for property management specifically put missed-call rates as high as 50 to 70%, among the highest of any service industry, largely because leasing offices simply aren't staffed for when demand actually shows up.

What typically happens without after-hours coverage

Three common setups, and where each one breaks down.

Voicemail. Most callers who reach voicemail don't leave a message. They move to the next available property on their list instead, and the leasing office never even knows the inquiry happened.

A generic answering service. Better than nothing, but most answering services work from a basic script. They can take a message, but they can't tell a caller what units are available, confirm pricing, or book a tour, so the call still ends without progress.

On-call staff rotations. This covers gaps, but unevenly. Coverage depends on whoever's on call that week, response quality varies person to person, and it puts a real burden on staff outside their working hours.

How an AI voice receptionist closes the gap

Leaseline answers every call, 24 hours a day, in under 1.5 seconds. It asks the same five questions on every call regardless of what time someone dials in, unit type, move-in timeline, budget, pets, and contact info, and books a tour on the spot if the caller qualifies. Nothing depends on which staff member happened to be on call that night. That consistency matters for more than just coverage; see how the same fixed process reduces fair housing exposure.

Every call gets logged to a dashboard automatically, so nothing from overnight or the weekend gets lost by the time the office opens Monday morning.

What this doesn't replace

An AI voice receptionist isn't a full leasing team, and it isn't meant to be. Leaseline doesn't handle maintenance requests, negotiate rent, or replace a property management system. Its job is narrow on purpose: answer the call, qualify the lead, and book the tour, so a human never has to do that specific job at 2 AM.
